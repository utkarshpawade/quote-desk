# Sample data

Everything here is generated by `npm run sample-data` (scripts/generate-sample-data.ts) from a
fixed random seed, so it is reproducible. All companies, people, part numbers, prices and drawings
are invented; customers are called Company B–H as in the brief.

## Past-quote register

| File | What it is | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| `past-quotes-2023-2026.xlsx` | 1,400 rows of the old Excel quote register, April 2023 – September 2026: quote no., date, customer, part, material, gross/net weight, annual qty, unit price, outcome, lost reason, routing as written, estimator. Prices were calculated with the real costing engine on the rate history in force on each date, plus ±4 % estimator judgement. | Loaded by the seed through the Import screen's service; "Import past quotes" → sample button |
| `past-quotes-with-errors.xlsx` | 60 rows with 7 deliberate mistakes: empty description, negative price, 31/02/2025, status "Maybe", net weight above gross, "approx 20k" as a quantity, a duplicated row | "Import past quotes" → sample button, to see the row report |

Three rows anchor the brief's sample parts: Q/24-25/0412 front wheel spindle (Company B),
Q/25-26/0233 hydraulic lift arm LH (Company C) and Q/25-26/0301 gear blank 42T (Company D).

## RFQ packs (`rfq-packs/`)

Each folder is one incoming RFQ as it would arrive: the email (`.eml`, with the other files as
attachments) and the attachments themselves. "New RFQ" → "Use this sample" loads a pack exactly as
a dropped email would be loaded.

| Pack | Contents | What it exercises |
|---|---|---|
| `company-d-gear-blank` | Email forwarded by sales, Excel part list, drawing PDF (DRV-GB-2207 rev C) | Unwrapping a forward to find the customer; values agreeing across email, Excel and title block. The source of RFQ-2611-023 in the demo data. |
| `company-e-brake-hub` | Email, Excel part list in grams, drawing PDF, photo of a title block | Gram-to-kg conversion, a monthly schedule annualised, a blank volume, a material conflict between Excel and email, an ambiguous date, an image read by the vision model |
| `company-f-yoke` | Short email | An unknown grade (SCM440) kept as text and flagged, and a prompt-injection line the extractor must ignore |

In the demo data Company G's NDA does not allow AI (`Customer.aiAllowed = false`): an email from
`@companyg.example` is read by the rule-based reader only, and the review screen says why.
